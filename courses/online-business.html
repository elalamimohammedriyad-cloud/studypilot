Start an Online Business

Learn the building blocks of a small online venture: offer, audience, storefront, operations and customer care.

Beginner6 hoursEN / FR

Course curriculum

Work through the modules in order. Each lesson includes a practical idea to apply.

Module 1: Choose an offer

Products and services

Compare digital products, physical products and services by skills, cost, delivery and customer needs.

Know your customer

Write a simple customer profile based on needs and behaviour, not stereotypes.

Module 2: Build the basics

Storefront essentials

A clear offer, honest description, contact method, policies and accessible design build trust.

Pricing and costs

Account for production, platform fees, delivery, taxes and customer support before setting a price.

Module 3: Operate responsibly

Customer experience

Set realistic delivery expectations, communicate clearly and handle complaints respectfully.

Measure and improve

Track a few useful measures such as visits, enquiries, conversion and repeat customers.

Practical project

Draft a launch plan for a hypothetical online offer. Include audience, offer, price assumptions, operating checklist and risks.

Knowledge check

1. What is the best first step?
2. How should you check quality?
3. What is a responsible habit?
