From Idea to Business

Turn an early idea into a tested concept with a clear audience, value proposition and simple plan.

Beginner5 hoursEN / FR

Course curriculum

Work through the modules in order. Each lesson includes a practical idea to apply.

Module 1: Find a real problem

Observe needs

Look for recurring frustrations in a community. Interview people respectfully and ask about their current solutions.

Define the audience

Describe who experiences the problem, in what situation and how often.

Module 2: Shape the solution

Value proposition

Explain the audience, the problem and the benefit in one clear sentence.

Simple prototype

Create a low-cost mock-up, sample page or demonstration before investing heavily.

Module 3: Test and plan

Gather feedback

Ask neutral questions, look for patterns and distinguish compliments from evidence of demand.

First action plan

List the next three actions, estimated costs, risks and a realistic way to measure progress.

Practical project

Prepare a two-page concept brief: problem, audience, proposed solution, evidence gathered, costs to investigate and next steps.

Knowledge check

1. What is the best first step?
2. How should you check quality?
3. What is a responsible habit?
