AI Skills for Beginners

Understand what modern AI can and cannot do, use it thoughtfully, and build a simple personal workflow.

Beginner3 hoursEN / FR

Course curriculum

Work through the modules in order. Each lesson includes a practical idea to apply.

Module 1: AI foundations

What AI is

AI systems find patterns in data and generate predictions or content. They do not think like people and can make confident mistakes.

Where AI helps

Explore writing support, brainstorming, translation, summarising and learning assistance. Check important facts independently.

Limits and responsible use

Protect private information, respect copyright, disclose AI assistance when required, and keep human judgment in control.

Module 2: Working with AI

Give useful context

State your goal, audience, background and constraints. Ask for an output format that is easy to use.

Improve an answer

Ask the tool to explain assumptions, identify uncertainty and revise a draft against clear criteria.

Module 3: Your first workflow

Apply the workflow

Choose one repetitive, low-risk task. Draft with AI, verify the result, edit it yourself and record what worked.

Practical project

Create a one-page guide showing how you would use AI for one learning or creative task, including a fact-checking step.

Knowledge check

1. What is the best first step?
2. How should you check quality?
3. What is a responsible habit?
