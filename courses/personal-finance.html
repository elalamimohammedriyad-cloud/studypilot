Personal Finance Essentials

Learn budgeting, saving, comparing costs and recognising common financial risks. Educational content, not individual financial advice.

Beginner4 hoursEN / FR

Course curriculum

Work through the modules in order. Each lesson includes a practical idea to apply.

Module 1: Understand your money

Income and expenses

Distinguish regular income, fixed expenses and variable spending. Use realistic estimates.

Build a simple budget

Plan for needs, goals and flexible spending. Review the plan and adjust it when circumstances change.

Module 2: Save and compare

Savings goals

Make goals specific and time-based. Small, consistent steps can help build a habit.

Compare total cost

Compare fees, terms, delivery costs and long-term commitments rather than looking only at the headline price.

Module 3: Recognise risks

Scams and pressure

Be cautious of guaranteed returns, urgency, requests for secrecy and offers that seem too good to be true.

Make informed choices

For important decisions, consult a trusted adult or qualified professional and check reliable local information.

Practical project

Create a sample monthly budget using fictional numbers, then explain three choices and one risk you would review.

Knowledge check

1. What is the best first step?
2. How should you check quality?
3. What is a responsible habit?
